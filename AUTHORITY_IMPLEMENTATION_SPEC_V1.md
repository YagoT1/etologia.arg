# AUTHORITY_IMPLEMENTATION_SPEC_V1

## Source and Scope Note

This specification converts Phase 1 findings into an authority-consolidation implementation specification for the **Hero → Professional → Method** sequence only. It uses `AUTHORITY_SPRINT_1_AUDIT_V1.md`, `AUTHORITY_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN_V1.md`, `PRODUCT_CONVERGENCE_REPORT_V1.md`, `AUTHORITY_POSITIONING_STRATEGY_V1.md`, `TRUST_ARCHITECTURE_FRAMEWORK_V1.md`, `UX_UI_SYSTEM_V1.md`, `IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP_V1.md`, and direct review of the actual codebase.

This is not a coding task. It does not generate code, redesign the website, implement changes, modify the stack, rewrite full sections, optimize conversion, or define later-phase trust architecture, UX/UI convergence, or premium polish.

The purpose is to define what must change, what must not change, what gains authority weight, what loses authority weight, and how Hero → Professional → Method becomes one unified authority system.

---

# Current State

## Current Authority Structure

The current authority structure is distributed across multiple valid signals rather than concentrated into a single authority system.

Authority currently appears in:

- the hero badge identifying “MV Agustina Gasparini · Etología clínica”;
- the hero headline and body copy connecting behavior problems to professional and human reading;
- the four hero authority signals: “Médica Veterinaria,” “Etología clínica,” “Evaluación conductual,” and “Plan familiar aplicable”;
- the hero visual overlay: “Mirada clínica, sin recetas universales”;
- the professional section's veterinary medicine and clinical ethology framing;
- the professional section's “Criterio clínico + sensibilidad familiar” block;
- the method section's clinical evaluation, plan definition, risk awareness, and follow-up sequence.

The homepage already contains strong authority material. The issue is not absence of authority. The issue is that authority is spread across several places and does not yet behave as the dominant organizing perception.

## Current Strengths

- The Hero names the professional and clinical field immediately.
- The Hero names serious concerns: anxiety, fear, aggression, stress, and difficult changes.
- The Professional Section grounds the service in veterinary medicine and clinical ethology.
- The Professional Section connects clinical criteria with family sensitivity.
- The Method Section contains strong clinical reasoning material, especially behavior, routine, environment, health, antecedents, family, emotional signs, veterinary control when needed, risk, feasibility, follow-up, and avoidance of universal solutions.
- The sequence is calm, humane, restrained, and emotionally safe.
- The current system avoids aggressive marketing, fear tactics, and over-medicalized coldness.

## Current Weaknesses

- The Hero carries too many jobs at once: authority, warmth, trust, accessibility, visual reassurance, and contact access.
- The Professional Section contains the strongest identity-based authority material but does not yet function as the dominant authority anchor.
- The Method Section contains the strongest evidence of clinical reasoning but can read first as process explanation.
- Hero authority signals are correct but lightweight and equal-weight.
- The sequence does not yet create a clear escalation from category orientation to authority anchor to authority proof.
- The website currently feels professional and clinically informed, but not yet fully established as a premium clinical authority platform.

---

# Target State

## Target Authority System

After Phase 1, the Hero → Professional → Method sequence should operate as one authority system with three distinct roles:

1. **Hero = Clinical Category Orientation**
2. **Professional Section = Authority Anchor**
3. **Method Section = Authority Proof**

The sequence should make users feel that the platform is not a warm service page with clinical language, but a calm premium clinical authority environment led by a qualified specialist.

## Target Journey

### Hero

The Hero should orient users immediately into the correct category: clinical behavioral judgment for dogs and cats in real family contexts. It should establish seriousness, specialist relevance, and professional confidence before accessibility becomes the dominant perception.

### Professional Section

The Professional Section should become the central authority anchor. It should carry the greatest identity-based authority weight because it is where professional qualification, clinical field, judgment, and the Human Clinical Premium balance are most naturally concentrated.

### Method Section

The Method Section should prove the authority established above. It should make clinical reasoning visible as the operational expression of the professional's expertise, rather than reading primarily as a service process.

## Target User Perception

The desired user perception is:

> “This is an established clinical authority environment. The professional is qualified, the category is specific, and the method shows responsible clinical judgment for complex behavior cases.”

# Hero Specification

## What Must Remain Unchanged

The following Hero elements should remain strategically stable during Phase 1:

- the presence of the professional/category identifier;
- the focus on behavior affecting the household;
- the recognition of anxiety, fear, aggression, stress, and difficult changes;
- the human and non-alarming tone;
- the idea that behavior is evaluated before a plan is built;
- the rejection of universal recipes;
- the calm, restrained, non-startup visual mood;
- basic access to the next step, without turning this phase into CTA optimization.

## What Must Gain Weight

The following Hero elements must gain authority weight:

- clinical category clarity;
- professional legitimacy;
- the idea of clinical behavioral judgment;
- case complexity;
- the seriousness of evaluation before guidance;
- the distinction from generic advice, training tips, and content education;
- the relationship between Hero promise and the professional authority that follows.

## What Must Lose Weight

The following Hero elements should lose relative authority dominance:

- immediate accessibility as the first remembered impression;
- emotional reassurance that is not explicitly tied to clinical authority;
- equal-weight chip-style authority signals;
- visual reassurance that does not strengthen clinical category clarity;
- any perception of the Hero as a generic service landing-page opener.

## What Must Move In Hierarchy

The hierarchy should move from:

1. warm problem recognition;
2. contact accessibility;
3. small authority signals;
4. clinical proof later.

To:

1. clinical category orientation;
2. professional authority relevance;
3. case complexity and judgment;
4. emotional safety;
5. access as a supported next step.

This does not require adding new sections or redesigning the Hero. It defines the authority order that Phase 1 must produce.

## What Currently Dilutes Authority

- The primary CTA and WhatsApp reassurance appear before authority has fully concentrated.
- Authority signals appear as small equal-weight cards rather than a hierarchy of clinical evidence.
- Warmth and accessibility can be perceived before expertise.
- The image overlay contains strong authority language but remains secondary to the main text path.
- The Hero is responsible for too many perceptions at once.

# Professional Section Specification

## What Must Remain Unchanged

The following Professional Section elements should remain strategically stable:

- the professional identity: MV Agustina Gasparini;
- the veterinary medicine foundation;
- the clinical ethology foundation;
- the non-blaming stance toward animal and family;
- the role of translating what is happening;
- the role of ordering priorities;
- the protection of the bond;
- the Human Clinical Premium balance of clinical criteria and family sensitivity;
- the recognition of pain, fear, learning, environment, handling, age, stress, and family dynamics.

## What Must Gain Weight

The following must gain authority weight:

- professional qualification as the central authority source;
- veterinary medicine and clinical ethology as the foundation of interpretation;
- clinical judgment as the primary value;
- case-complexity competence;
- risk prevention and realistic guidance;
- the professional's role as an interpreter of complexity, not only an accompanier;
- the bridge between professional authority and the method that follows.

## What Must Lose Weight

The following should lose relative weight:

- the perception of the section as a biographical or accompaniment module;
- generic warmth not explicitly tied to clinical judgment;
- modality details as a primary authority signal;
- equal visual/strategic weight with surrounding supporting modules;
- any reading of the professional section as personal reassurance rather than clinical authority.

## What Must Become Dominant

The Professional Section must become the **authority anchor** of the Hero → Professional → Method journey.

The dominant message should be:

> “This professional has the clinical background and judgment to interpret complex dog and cat behavior in real family contexts.”

This section should not become colder, credential-heavy, self-promotional, or institutional. It should become more authoritative by clarifying the relationship between professional role, clinical judgment, and case complexity.

## What Currently Dilutes Authority

- The section is structurally similar to other card-based modules.
- The label “Quién te acompaña” frames the section first through accompaniment rather than authority.
- The strongest clinical-complexity idea appears inside a supporting block rather than defining the section's total perception.
- “Enfoque” and “Modalidad” are useful but secondary; they do not carry enough authority weight to anchor the section.
- The section supports the Hero instead of becoming the authority center.

# Method Section Specification

## What Must Remain Unchanged

The following Method Section elements should remain stable:

- the existence of a clear method;
- the sequence from initial case description to evaluation, plan, and follow-up;
- the clinical evaluation of behavior, routine, environment, health, antecedents, family, and emotional signs;
- the boundary that veterinary control is indicated when needed;
- family feasibility and real-life constraints;
- attention to risks and level of accompaniment;
- follow-up, adjustment, doubts, and progress measurement;
- rejection of magic solutions and universal recipes.

## What Must Gain Weight

The following method elements must gain authority weight:

- clinical reasoning;
- evaluation before prescription;
- health and context screening;
- risk and safety criteria;
- case-specific hypothesis formation;
- professional judgment across the entire process;
- the idea that the method is the operational expression of the professional's authority.

## What Must Lose Weight

The following should lose relative weight:

- process-as-convenience;
- WhatsApp intake as the defining first impression of the method;
- step sequencing as generic workflow;
- family calm as the primary read when not tied to clinical reasoning;
- equal importance across all steps when the clinical evaluation step carries greater authority value.

## What Must Become More Visible

The following must become more visible in the method's authority perception:

- that the method is not a checklist;
- that clinical evaluation precedes intervention;
- that behavior is interpreted through multiple factors;
- that medical contributors are considered when relevant;
- that safety, risk, feasibility, and follow-up are part of professional judgment;
- that the method proves the professional's authority rather than merely explaining the service.

## What Currently Dilutes Authority

- The method begins with WhatsApp intake, making accessibility visible before clinical reasoning.
- The process card structure can read as a standard service workflow.
- Step 2 contains the strongest authority material, but it is one step among four rather than the defining perception.
- The title emphasizes anxiety reduction and decision-making, which supports trust, but does not make clinical judgment the first read.
- The Method Section is separated from the Professional Section by the Consultation Section in the current page flow, weakening the direct Professional → Method authority arc.

# Authority Hierarchy Specification

## Official Phase 1 Hierarchy

The official Phase 1 authority hierarchy is:

1. **Hero — Category Orientation**
2. **Professional Section — Authority Anchor**
3. **Method Section — Authority Proof**

## Hero

### Strategic Role

The Hero's strategic role is to make users immediately understand that the platform belongs to a serious clinical behavior category, not training advice, generic wellness, or content education.

### Visual Role

The Hero should remain the first visual orientation point, but it should not carry all authority responsibility alone.

### Authority Role

The Hero should establish enough authority for users to continue with confidence. It should introduce clinical seriousness, specialist relevance, and case complexity, then hand authority leadership to the Professional Section.

## Professional Section

### Strategic Role

The Professional Section's strategic role is to anchor authority in the qualified specialist.

### Visual Role

It should carry more strategic weight than ordinary supporting sections. It should not feel like a standard card module or biographical aside.

### Authority Role

It should become the central authority source in the sequence by connecting veterinary medicine, clinical ethology, case complexity, judgment, and family sensitivity.

## Method Section

### Strategic Role

The Method Section's strategic role is to demonstrate that authority is operational and structured.

### Visual Role

It should read as evidence of clinical reasoning, not only as process explanation.

### Authority Role

It should prove that the professional's authority results in a disciplined evaluation-and-guidance model.

# Success Criteria

Phase 1 succeeds if the Hero → Professional → Method sequence produces measurable authority improvements without redesign, conversion optimization, or trust-architecture implementation.

## Perception Criteria

- Users can identify the platform as a clinical behavior authority within the first 10 seconds.
- Users can identify the professional as the central authority source within the first 60 seconds.
- Users can identify the method as structured clinical reasoning within the first 3 minutes.
- The platform feels more established without feeling colder.
- The sequence feels less like separate modules and more like one authority journey.

## Hierarchy Criteria

- Hero functions as category orientation, not the entire authority system.
- Professional Section functions as authority anchor, not supporting biography.
- Method Section functions as authority proof, not generic workflow.
- Authority and expertise lead before warmth/accessibility in the remembered sequence.

## Content Criteria

- Clinical judgment is more prominent than generic reassurance.
- Veterinary medicine and clinical ethology are clearly connected to case interpretation.
- Case complexity is visible and organized.
- Method reasoning is more visible than process mechanics.
- Accessibility remains available but does not dominate authority perception.

## Risk Criteria

Phase 1 fails if:

- warmth increases while authority remains distributed;
- more credentials are added without hierarchy;
- the Professional Section remains underweighted;
- the Method Section remains primarily procedural;
- CTA/accessibility cues become more dominant;
- the site becomes colder or more institutional;
- the sequence still feels professional but not established.

# Final Recommendation

If only three authority improvements were executed, the three highest-impact improvements would be the following.

## 1. Make The Professional Section The Authority Anchor

### Why

The Professional Section contains the strongest identity-based authority material: professional name, veterinary medicine, clinical ethology, clinical criteria, sensitivity to family context, risk prevention, and realistic guidance. It is currently important content, but not yet the authority center.

### Impact

This directly solves the most important Phase 1 gap: authority exists but is not institutionalized.

## 2. Make The Method Section Read As Clinical Reasoning Proof

### Why

The Method Section contains the strongest operational evidence of expertise. It shows evaluation, health/context awareness, family reality, risk, follow-up, and rejection of universal recipes. Its authority value should be proof of judgment, not process explanation.

### Impact

This transforms authority from a claim into a visible professional reasoning system.

## 3. Reorder The Hero's Authority Perception

### Why

The Hero determines the first 10-second interpretation. It already contains the right elements, but the perceived order can lean toward warmth/accessibility before authority/expertise.

### Impact

This ensures users enter the journey through clinical category and professional confidence before they interpret the platform as a warm service page.

## Final Specification Statement

Phase 1 should not add more sections, add more badges, redesign the homepage, optimize CTAs, or introduce new visual systems.

Phase 1 should concentrate existing authority.

The required transformation is:

> Hero orients the user into a serious clinical category. Professional Section anchors authority in the qualified specialist. Method Section proves that authority through structured clinical reasoning.

When this relationship is clear, the platform can move from “professional and warm” to “established premium clinical authority” without losing Human Clinical Premium, emotional safety, or product maturity.
