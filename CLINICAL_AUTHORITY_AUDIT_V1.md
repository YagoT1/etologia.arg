# 1. CORE QUESTION

# =========================================================

The audit must answer:

# Does the website communicate a warm professional?

or

# Does the website communicate a clinical specialist?

This distinction is critical.

The final product must be perceived as:

* clinically competent
* professionally established
* emotionally intelligent
* trustworthy
* specialized

NOT simply:

* friendly
* approachable
* empathetic
