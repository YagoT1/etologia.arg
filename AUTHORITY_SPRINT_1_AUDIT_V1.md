# Current Authority Journey

## Current Journey Sequence

The current homepage authority journey begins with a calm hero, moves into a professional identity section, and then later reaches a method section after an intervening case-recognition and consultation section.

The relevant authority sequence is:

1. **Hero:** establishes professional identity, clinical category, problem seriousness, emotional safety, and immediate access.
2. **Professional Section:** explains the professional role through veterinary medicine, clinical ethology, non-blaming interpretation, family sensitivity, and clinical criteria.
3. **Method Section:** explains the working process through intake, evaluation, practical planning, and follow-up.

## What Users Experience Today

### Hero Experience

The hero creates a first impression of a professional, warm, clinically oriented service. It names “MV Agustina Gasparini · Etología clínica,” frames behavior as something that can change the life of the household, names anxiety, fear, aggression, stress, and difficult changes, and states that the work evaluates what behavior is communicating before building a safe and respectful plan.

The hero also introduces a practical access layer quickly through WhatsApp and a secondary link to the consultation section. The four small authority signals — “Médica Veterinaria,” “Etología clínica,” “Evaluación conductual,” and “Plan familiar aplicable” — reinforce relevance but do not create a dominant authority moment.

### Professional Section Experience

The professional section deepens the clinical basis by stating that the work comes from veterinary medicine and clinical ethology. It defines the professional role as translating what is happening, ordering priorities, and caring for the bond while addressing the problem.

This section contains strong authority substance, especially in the “Criterio clínico + sensibilidad familiar” block, where behavior is linked to pain, fear, learning, environment, handling, age, stress, and family dynamics. However, the section reads as one module among several rather than the authority center of the platform.

### Method Section Experience

The method section presents a clear four-step process. It includes practical intake, clinical evaluation, plan definition, and follow-up. The strongest authority signal appears in step 2, which explicitly references behavior, routine, environment, health, antecedents, family, emotional signs, and veterinary control when needed.

The section demonstrates judgment, but its first read is also procedural and accessible. Because the sequence begins with “Me contás qué está pasando” via WhatsApp, users may initially perceive the method as a guided service process before they perceive it as a clinical reasoning model.

## What Perception Is Created?

The sequence currently creates this perception:

> “This is a professional, warm, clinically informed service led by a veterinarian/ethology professional who understands family context and can guide behavior concerns responsibly.”

That perception is positive and strategically aligned, but it is not yet strong enough to fully communicate:

> “This is an established premium clinical authority environment for complex dog and cat behavior cases.”

## What Perception Is Missing?

The missing perception is **institutionalized clinical authority**.

Specifically, the current sequence does not yet make the user feel, with enough force, that:

- authority is the organizing principle of the experience;
- professional judgment is the primary value;
- the platform is differentiated from training advice, generic behavioral guidance, wellness content, or social media education;
- the professional section is the central authority anchor;
- the method is a clinical reasoning system, not only a clear process;
- the Hero → Professional → Method sequence is one escalating authority arc rather than three valid but partially independent sections.

# Desired Authority Journey

## Strategic Authority Standard

The ideal authority journey should make users feel that they are entering a calm, established clinical practice environment led by a qualified specialist. Warmth should make authority emotionally safe, but warmth should not be the dominant perception.

The desired sequence is:

1. **Hero:** “This is the correct clinical category and the right kind of professional environment.”
2. **Professional Section:** “This authority is anchored in a qualified professional with clinical judgment, not generic advice.”
3. **Method Section:** “This professional has a structured reasoning model for complex behavior cases.”

## After 10 Seconds

Users should think:

- “This is a clinical behavior authority, not a casual pet advice page.”
- “The professional category is clear.”
- “The site understands serious behavior concerns without dramatizing them.”
- “The tone is calm, but the service is not lightweight.”
- “It is worth continuing because this appears clinically credible.”

The 10-second goal is **orientation authority**: category clarity, seriousness, and professional legitimacy.

## After 60 Seconds

Users should think:

- “This professional understands behavior as a complex clinical and family-context problem.”
- “The work is grounded in veterinary medicine and clinical ethology.”
- “The professional is not offering generic tips or obedience advice.”
- “The case will be interpreted with judgment, context, and boundaries.”
- “This feels more specialized than a warm service provider.”

The 60-second goal is **professional authority recognition**: the user understands who is qualified to guide the case and why.

## After 3 Minutes

Users should think:

- “There is a method behind the service.”
- “The method evaluates behavior, health, context, family reality, risk, and follow-up.”
- “This is not an instant solution or universal recipe.”
- “The professional can guide a real case responsibly.”
- “This platform has enough maturity and clinical seriousness to rely on.”

The 3-minute goal is **clinical-reliance authority**: the user believes the professional can responsibly interpret and guide a complex case.

**7 / 10**

- The headline frames the problem as behavior affecting the life of the household, raising the seriousness beyond casual pet content.

- The hero communicates authority, trust, warmth, and accessibility at the same time, but authority does not clearly dominate the hierarchy.

- The primary action appears early, which may shift first perception toward service accessibility before authority has fully concentrated.
- The headline emphasizes “lectura profesional y humana,” which is aligned, but the clinical authority layer is less forceful than the emotional-human layer.
- The visual authority statement in the image overlay is secondary and partially detached from the main text hierarchy.

## Dilution Factors

### Moderate Dilution

- Early contact availability can make the hero feel like a high-quality service landing page instead of an established authority environment.
- The trust-signal grid spreads authority across four small boxes instead of concentrating it.
- The hero balances warmth and authority, but warmth/accessibility may be perceived first by some users.

### Minor Dilution

- The image overlay adds useful clinical language but competes with the primary authority path because it is visually separate from the main text.
- The phrase “Podés escribirme aunque no sepas cómo nombrar el problema” increases emotional safety but also pulls the hero toward accessibility.

## Missed Opportunities

- The hero does not fully convert the existing clinical signals into one dominant category perception.
- The hero does not yet make the professional's authority feel institutionalized before introducing action.
- The hero does not fully distinguish the platform from training advice, generic wellness, or content education in the first read.
- The hero does not make “clinical behavioral judgment” the unmistakable first-order value.

## Authority Impact

**High**

The hero determines whether users initially enter the journey through authority or through warmth/accessibility. It currently succeeds at professional clarity but does not yet fully establish premium clinical authority as the dominant perception.

1. **Authority**

# Authority Hierarchy Analysis

## Current Hierarchy

The current hierarchy is balanced but not authority-dominant.

### 1. Hero Currently Dominates

The hero dominates because it is first, visually largest, and carries the highest-density combination of identity, emotional problem, contact access, trust signals, and image support.

### 2. Professional Section Supports

The Professional Section supports the hero with credibility and clinical context, but it does not surpass the hero as the central authority anchor.

### 3. Method Section Explains

The Method Section explains how the work happens and includes clinical reasoning, but it arrives as a process module rather than the culmination of a clearly escalating authority arc.

## Which Section Currently Dominates?

**Hero**

The Hero dominates perception because of placement, visual prominence, headline scale, immediate CTAs, trust chips, and image composition.

## Which Section Should Dominate?

**The relationship between Professional Section and Method Section should dominate the authority journey.**

The Hero should orient. The Professional Section should anchor authority. The Method Section should prove authority through clinical reasoning. The strongest authority perception should emerge from the Professional → Method relationship, not from the Hero alone.

## Which Section Is Underweighted?

**Professional Section**

It contains the most important identity-based authority signals but currently feels like important supporting content rather than the authority center.

## Which Section Is Overweighted?

**Hero as an all-purpose perception container**

The Hero carries category, warmth, trust, action, and authority simultaneously. Because it has to do so much, authority becomes one of several signals rather than the dominant organizing impression.

## Hierarchy Diagnosis

The current hierarchy is:

1. Hero as broad orientation and action gateway.
2. Professional Section as credibility support.
3. Method Section as process explanation.

The desired authority hierarchy is:

1. Hero as clinical category orientation.
2. Professional Section as authority anchor.
3. Method Section as authority proof.

# Risk Analysis

## 1. Increasing Warmth Over Authority

Phase 1 could fail if the response to the audit is to make the sequence more emotionally reassuring rather than more clinically authoritative. Warmth must support authority, not replace it.

## 2. Increasing Accessibility Over Expertise

The current sequence already makes contact and orientation accessible. Increasing accessibility too early could make the platform feel service-provider direct rather than clinically established.

## 3. Introducing Credentials Without Hierarchy

Adding more credentials without changing the authority hierarchy would create more proof but not more authority. It could make the experience feel defensive or badge-heavy.

## 4. Flattening Section Hierarchy

If Hero, Professional, and Method remain visually and structurally similar in weight, authority will stay distributed. Equal weight is not maturity; it is hierarchy dilution.

## 5. Making The Professional Section Too Personal

The professional section must not become personality-led. Human credibility matters, but the section's role is clinical authority, not personal affinity.

## 6. Making The Method Too Procedural

If the method is treated as a service workflow, it will not prove expertise. Its authority value comes from clinical reasoning, not from the fact that it has steps.

## 7. Over-Correcting Into Cold Clinical Tone

Authority consolidation must not make the site institutional, distant, or medicalized. The target is Human Clinical Premium, not corporate medical coldness.

## 8. Treating CTA Presence As The Main Problem

CTA optimization belongs to a later phase. In Sprint 1, contact prompts should only be considered insofar as they affect authority perception.

## 9. Solving Authority Through Visual Decoration

Authority should not be solved through icons, decorative panels, aggressive contrast, motion, or trend effects. The issue is hierarchy and concentration, not decoration.

## 10. Ignoring The Relationship Between Sections

The biggest mistake would be auditing Hero, Professional, and Method independently only. The authority problem is systemic: the three sections do not yet form a strong enough authority arc.

# Executive Verdict

## Single Most Important Conclusion

The single most important conclusion is:

> The Hero, Professional Section, and Method Section each contain credible authority material, but they do not yet operate as one concentrated authority system.

This is why the website currently feels **professional but not yet an established premium clinical authority platform**.

The Hero creates professional warmth and clinical relevance. The Professional Section provides real authority substance but does not dominate as the authority anchor. The Method Section contains strong evidence of clinical reasoning but is read partly as process explanation. Together, the sequence communicates competence and care, but not yet the full perception of an established premium clinical authority environment.

## If Only One Area Could Be Improved During Phase 1

If only one area could be improved during Phase 1, it should be:

> **the relationship between Hero → Professional Section → Method Section.**

## Why The Relationship Matters More Than Any Single Section

Improving only the Hero would strengthen the first impression but could leave authority unsupported after the scroll.

Improving only the Professional Section would strengthen the authority anchor but could leave the Hero and Method disconnected from that authority.

Improving only the Method Section would strengthen proof of expertise but could still make authority feel discovered late rather than established early.

The highest-impact authority opportunity is therefore the relationship between the three sections:

- Hero should orient users into the clinical category.
- Professional Section should anchor the clinical authority.
- Method Section should prove that authority through structured clinical reasoning.

This relationship is the core Phase 1 authority system. Until it is clarified, later trust, UX/UI convergence, conversion, and polish work may improve the experience but will not solve the central strategic problem: authority exists, but it is distributed rather than institutionalized.
